assistant-hub

A private, self-hosted personal assistant used by one person — its owner. It is not a public service and does not accept other users.

What it does

  • Reads the owner's Gmail to spot bills, due dates, subscriptions and notable messages.
  • Sends the owner short summaries and reminders in a private Telegram chat.
  • Prepares email drafts for the owner to review and send manually. It cannot send or delete email.

Google access

  • gmail.readonly — to read messages.
  • gmail.compose — to create drafts only.
